# Privacy Policy for MindSync

**Last Updated: April 28, 2026**

MindSync ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
MindSync is designed with a "Privacy First" architecture. 
- **No Personal Data Collection**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, such as your name, email address, location, or contact details.
- **Offline Processing**: All AI-powered features (summarization, content generation, etc.) are performed entirely **offline** on your device. Your text inputs and the AI-generated outputs never leave your device and are not uploaded to any servers.

## 2. Permissions
MindSync requests the following permissions for specific technical reasons:
- **Internet Access**: Used solely to download the AI model files (GGUF) required for the app to function. Once the model is downloaded, the app can function entirely without an internet connection.
- **Foreground Service**: Used to ensure that large model downloads or intensive AI processing tasks are not interrupted by the operating system.
- **Notifications**: Used to provide updates on the status of model downloads or task completion.

## 3. Third-Party Services
MindSync does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect it in the first place.

## 4. Data Security
Since all data remains on your device, the security of your information depends on your device's security settings. We recommend using standard device security features (passcodes, biometric locks) to protect your data.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
